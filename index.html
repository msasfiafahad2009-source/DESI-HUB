SECRET MASTERY

The Chef's Royal Secrets

Mastering traditional Pakistani cuisine requires respect for ancestral techniques:

  • Freshly Ground Spices

    Always dry roast whole spices right before grinding to release essential aromatic oils.

  • Patient Onions (Bhunai)

    Cook sliced onions slowly until rich golden brown. This builds the caramelized base for curries.

  • Precise Measurements

    Balance intense chili heat with fresh yogurt or whole milk to keep meat succulent.

  • The Final Dum & Herbs

    Seal your cooking pot on low heat for the final 10 minutes and garnish with fresh herbs.
